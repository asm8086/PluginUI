Hosting audio plug-ins from your own code, without a user interface: <xref:PluginUI.Host.PluginLoader> opens a plug-in
by name or path, <xref:PluginUI.Host.PluginInstance> is a loaded plug-in of any format (buses, parameters, MIDI in and
out, processing with host buffers, state, <xref:PluginUI.Host.PluginEditor>). See [Plug-in host](../../docs/PLUGIN_HOST.md).
