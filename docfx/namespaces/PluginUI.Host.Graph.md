The modular graph: <xref:PluginUI.Host.Graph.PluginGraph> plays modules (<xref:PluginUI.Host.Graph.GraphNode>) joined by
cables (<xref:PluginUI.Host.Graph.GraphConnection>), live on an audio device or offline. Modules for plug-ins
(<xref:PluginUI.Host.Graph.PluginNode>), device inputs and outputs, files, test signals, keyboards, MIDI ports, gains,
sums, muxers and demuxers; loops with a feedback delay, latency compensation in the sums, graph files with
<xref:PluginUI.Host.Graph.PluginReference> to find the plug-ins again.
